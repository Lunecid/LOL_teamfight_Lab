uses, and this section outlines the game and its match data.

Summoner's Rift, lane, jungle, river &
  The map: bases in opposite corners joined by three \emph{lanes}; the \emph{jungle} between them holds
  neutral monster camps; the \emph{river} joins the other two corners, with the Baron pit in its north-west
  half~\cite{lolwiki2025summonersrift,lolwiki2026baron}. Drake kills in the data place the dragon pit in
  its south-east half. \\

Team, side, champion, role &
  Five players per team, the ten participants of each match record~\cite{riotapi2024matchv5}; \emph{blue}
  based in the south-west and \emph{red} in the
  north-east~\cite{lolwiki2025summonersrift}; base coordinates make blue team 100 in the data. Each player
  controls a \emph{champion}, a character with its own abilities~\cite{lolwiki2026champion}, in a role:
  top, jungle, middle, bottom or support. \\
% C3 (coordinate ranges, 900 sampled matches); header note (orientation).
Game unit (u) &
  Unit of map coordinates $(x, y)$: blue base near the origin, $x$ east, $y$ north; in 900 sampled
  matches every coordinate lies in 0--14,800\,u. \\

% lolwiki2026minion: periodic waves from each Nexus along the lanes.  lolwiki2026farming (redirect Creep

Minion, creep score (CS) &
  Minions are computer-controlled units that each Nexus sends down every lane in
  waves~\cite{lolwiki2026minion}. CS counts the minions and neutral monsters a player has
  killed~\cite{lolwiki2026farming}, reported as lane CS (\texttt{minionsKilled}) and jungle CS
  (\texttt{jungleMinionsKilled}). \\

Gold, experience (XP), level &
  Gold, earned passively and by killing minions, monsters, champions, plates and structures, buys
  items~\cite{lolwiki2026gold}. XP from a champion's death goes to enemy champions within 1,600\,u (our
  constant $R$); XP raises a champion's level, up to 18~\cite{lolwiki2026experience}. \\
% lolwiki2026kill: final blow; credit window 15 s on Summoner's Rift, no patch entry.  lolwiki2026assist:
%   15 s, no patch history.  B = 15 s: manifest.json TF2_ENGAGE_PRE_KILL_MS 15000.  lolwiki2026terminology

Kill, assist, ace &
  A \emph{kill} brings an enemy champion's health to zero (\texttt{CHAMPION\_KILL}: killer, victim,
  assisters, position). An \emph{assist} credits an ally that affected the victim in its last
  15\,s~\cite{lolwiki2026kill,lolwiki2026assist}, our constant $B$. An \emph{ace} leaves every champion
  of one team dead~\cite{lolwiki2026terminology} (\texttt{KILL\_ACE}). \\
% lolwiki2026death: respawn at the fountain; base wait 10 s (level 1) to 52.5 s (level 18); longer after
%   15 minutes.  No patch-history section on the page (header note); no change listed in 25.14-25.16.
Death timer &
  A killed champion is out of play until it respawns in its base: 10\,s at level 1 to 52.5\,s at level
  18, and longer after 15\,min of play~\cite{lolwiki2026death}. \\
% lolwiki2026bounties + riot2025patch2509: 300 g at levels 1-6, +10 g per level from 7 to 18, max 420 g
%   (set in 25.09; no base change in the 25.10-25.16 notes).  Shutdown gold accrues from kills, assists and
%   farming.  riot2025patch2514 / lolwiki2026v2514: bounty from farming 1 per 17.5 g -> 1 per 20 g,
%   suppression thresholds 25 % wider.  Corpus: 300 is the most frequent CHAMPION_KILL bounty in S900
%   (2,760 / 2,764 / 2,742 kills per patch).  Label use: gameplay/labels.py _compute_label_market_event.
Bounty, shutdown gold &
  Gold for a champion kill: a base of 300 at levels 1--6, rising to 420 at level 18 (since 25.09), plus the
  shutdown gold the victim accrued from kills, assists and, more slowly since 25.14,
  farming~\cite{lolwiki2026bounties,riot2025patch2509,riot2025patch2514}; kill events record both
  (\texttt{bounty}, \texttt{shutdownBounty}). \\

Turret, plating &
  Each team has an outer, an inner and an inhibitor turret (\texttt{BASE\_TURRET}) in every lane and two
  nexus turrets. In these patches only outer turrets had \emph{plating}: five plates paying gold when
  destroyed (\texttt{TURRET\_PLATE\_DESTROYED}) and falling off at
  14:00~\cite{lolwiki2026turret,riot2026patch261}. \\

%   spawns minions; invulnerable while all three allied inhibitors or at least one nexus turret stand;

Inhibitor, Nexus &
  An \emph{inhibitor} stands behind each inhibitor turret; for the 5\,min until it respawns, enemy waves
  in its lane include super minions~\cite{lolwiki2026inhibitor}. Destroying the enemy \emph{Nexus} wins
  the match; it is vulnerable only once its two nexus turrets and at least one of its inhibitors are
  down~\cite{lolwiki2026nexus}. \\

Elemental dragon (drake), Dragon Soul, Elder Dragon &
  A drake of one of six elements spawns in the dragon pit at 5:00 and 5\,min after each kill, and gives
  the killing team a permanent buff of its element; a team's fourth drake also grants the \emph{Dragon Soul}
  (\texttt{DRAGON\_SOUL\_GIVEN})~\cite{lolwiki2026dragonpit,lolwiki2026dragonslayer}. The \emph{Elder
  Dragon} then spawns, and again 6\,min after each kill; its 150\,s buff burns enemies and executes enemy champions below 20\,\% of
  maximum health~\cite{lolwiki2026elderdragon,lolwiki2026dragonslayer}. \\

Voidgrubs, Rift Herald &

  (\texttt{RIFTHERALD}), which drops an item that summons it to charge enemy
  turrets~\cite{lolwiki2026voidgrub,lolwiki2026riftherald,lolwiki2026v2509}. \\

Atakhan &
  An epic monster from patch 25.S1.1 until its removal in 26.1, so present in all three patches studied.
  It spawns at 20:00 at the end of the river in the half of the map that saw more champion combat by
  14:00, and gives the killing team a lasting buff~\cite{lolwiki2026atakhan,riot2026patch261}. \\

Baron Nashor &
  Spawns in the Baron pit at 25:00 in these patches (20:00 before 25.S1.1 and from 26.1) and 6\,min after
  each kill; its 180\,s buff strengthens nearby allied minions and speeds the return to
  base~\cite{lolwiki2026baron}. \\

Vision, ward, control ward &
  A team sees only what its units see, a champion 1,350\,u around it; the rest is under the \emph{fog of
  war}. A \emph{ward}, invisible to enemies, reveals 900\,u; a \emph{control ward} is visible, reveals and disables enemy
  wards, one per player~\cite{lolwiki2026sight,lolwiki2025ward} (\texttt{WARD\_PLACED},
  \texttt{WARD\_KILL}). \\

Match-V5 match record, frames &
  The match record lists the ten participants (champion, team, role) and the
  result~\cite{riotapi2024matchv5}. The timeline~\cite{riotapi2024timeline} has a \emph{participant frame}
  about every 60\,s (60.00--60.17\,s in the corpus) with each player's position, level, XP, gold, CS, champion
  statistics and damage totals. \\

Match-V5 events &
  Millisecond-timestamped records of kills, aces, plates, structures, epic monsters, Dragon Souls, wards,
  items and level-ups. Only kill, plate, structure and monster events have a position; none records
  movement or damage, except a summary of each kill victim's damage. \\

\subsection{The Game and Its Patches}\label{sec:background-game}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

% Five per team: C1 and riotapi2024matchv5 (participants with teamId; the count ten is the corpus's, see the Team row comment).
%   lolwiki2026nexus supports only the win condition.  lolwiki2026gold, lolwiki2026experience;
%   lolwiki2026kill (bounty to the killer), lolwiki2026death.  Scoreboard reference: sec_learners.tex
%   paragraph "Scoreboard reference" (label sec:learners-ladder).
Two teams of five players, the ten participants of each match record~\cite{riotapi2024matchv5}, meet on
Summoner's Rift, and a team wins by destroying the enemy Nexus~\cite{lolwiki2026nexus}. Champions grow stronger with gold and experience, so a team that leads
in both tends to field stronger champions~\cite{lolwiki2026gold,lolwiki2026experience}; that lead is the
reference every learner in this paper must beat (Section~\ref{sec:learners-ladder}). A kill pays the
killing side and leaves the victim's team a player short until the death timer
ends~\cite{lolwiki2026kill,lolwiki2026death}, while structures and epic monsters reward the team that
takes them, so the outcome label values an engagement by everything each side takes.

% Mapping and dates: header note "Patch numbering relied on".
Riot Games changes the rules in \emph{patches}. The corpus covers three, numbered 15.14--15.16 in the
Match-V5 \texttt{gameVersion} field and 25.14--25.16 in Riot's notes (July and August
2025~\cite{lolwiki2026v2514,lolwiki2026v2516}): since 25.S1.1 the client shows year-based
numbers~\cite{riot2025patch25s11}, and Yunara, introduced in 25.14~\cite{riot2025patch2514}, first
appears in Riot's data at version 15.14.1~\cite{riot2025ddragon1514}. Patch 26.1 (January 2026)
removed Atakhan and made plates permanent~\cite{riot2026patch261}, so the current game differs from
Table~\ref{tab:terms}.

\subsection{The Data}\label{sec:background-data}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

% Frames ~60 s: C2.  Events, positions, no damage or movement events: C3.  How old the last frame is at
%   the cutoff is measured in sec_limitations.tex ("What the inputs observe"); not repeated here.
%   "Champion kills ... between champions": fact-check 2026-09-14 -- plate, building and elite-monster
%   events also carry a position and a millisecond timestamp (C3), but none records champion-versus-
%   champion combat; CHAMPION_SPECIAL_KILL (ace, multikill, first blood) carries a position too but, in C5,
%   always shares timestamp and killer with a CHAMPION_KILL (6,700 of 6,700).
Match-V5 timelines hold two streams of different resolution~\cite{riotapi2024timeline}: participant
frames about once a minute and events to the millisecond (Table~\ref{tab:terms}). What the events
record is therefore known exactly, but where the champions stand and how strong they are is known only
as of the last frame, which may be almost a minute old
(Section~\ref{subsec:limitations}). Champion kills are the only record of combat between champions that
is both timestamped to the millisecond and placed on the map. Section~\ref{sec:definition} therefore
builds engagements from kills, and fights in which nobody dies lie outside what it can detect
(Section~\ref{sec:def-killless}).
